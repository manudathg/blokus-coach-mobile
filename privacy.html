Privacy Policy

Corner Tiles Kids Coach
Last updated: February 22, 2026

Corner Tiles Kids Coach is designed for family play and learning. We keep privacy simple: the app runs locally on your device and does not require account sign-in.

Data We Collect

We do not collect personal information such as name, email, phone number, or precise location.

Tracking and Ads

We do not use third-party advertising SDKs and we do not track you across apps or websites.

Analytics

We do not use analytics services that collect personal data.

Local Data

Gameplay state and settings may be stored locally on your device to support the app experience. This data stays on your device and is not uploaded to our servers.

Children's Privacy

The app is intended to be kid-friendly and family-safe. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and revise the "Last updated" date.

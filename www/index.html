Core Rules 📘

First Piece

Player 1 must cover top-left corner. Player 2 must cover bottom-right corner.

Corner Contact

Your new piece must touch one of your existing pieces diagonally.

No Edge Contact

Your own pieces may not share an edge. Opponent edge contact is allowed.

Endgame

Game ends when both players cannot move. Higher tile count wins.
